// how we work

Five rounds. No
surprise invoices.

We work in fixed phases on a scoped quote. You see the studio brief in week one, the first design rounds in week three, and a launched site somewhere between week six and eight.

  1. 01 · Listen

    Listen

    Week 1

    We start at the desk you actually work from.

    Two long conversations. We learn how you sound to a referral, what your worst client taught you, and what you want to be three years from now.

    • Founder interview
    • Audience read-throughs
    • Audit of current touchpoints
  2. 02 · Frame

    Frame

    Weeks 2–3

    Positioning, voice, and the shape of the work.

    We write the studio brief — one page, no decks. It names the audience, the shape of the brand, and the three things the website has to earn.

    • Studio brief (1 page)
    • Voice document
    • Sitemap and message hierarchy
  3. 03 · Design

    Design

    Weeks 3–6

    Identity and screens — drawn together, not in series.

    Brand system and website design happen on the same canvas. A wordmark earns its weight by working at 11pt in a footer and 110pt on a vehicle.

    • Mark, palette, typography
    • Page-by-page art direction
    • Two rounds of revisions
  4. 04 · Ship

    Ship

    Weeks 6–8

    Built, tested, indexed, handed over.

    Built to modern performance standards, tuned for Core Web Vitals, structured for AI search and Google. We hand over a site you can update without us.

    • Performance + accessibility pass
    • SEO + AEO setup
    • Owner training
  5. 05 · Tend

    Tend

    Ongoing (optional)

    Quarterly rounds keep the work earning its keep.

    Most owners stay on a small monthly retainer for content updates, evergreen SEO, and a quarterly health check. No tickets, no surprise invoices.

    • Quarterly content rounds
    • Performance + AEO maintenance
    • Direct line to the founder
